ABOUT AAPDA BUDDY

Your friend in orbit, watching over you.

Aapda Buddy is a concept platform for the AI & Machine Learning for Space Data Analytics track. It turns freely available satellite imagery into a simple operational picture for disaster-management teams. The goal is not to replace human experts—it is to help them decide where to look first.

Designed as a hackathon prototype using public sources such as Sentinel, Landsat and MODIS. All incident visuals on this site are simulated demonstration data.
01

Ingest

Collect optical, thermal and radar observations, then align them by location and time.

02

Detect

A segmentation model highlights floodwater, burn scars and unusual vegetation change.

03

Verify

Confidence scores and historical comparisons reduce false alarms before escalation.

04

Coordinate

Responders receive a location, severity level, affected-area estimate and trend.

Terms & responsible use

Clear limits are part of trustworthy disaster intelligence.

TERMS OF USE

Decision support, not an emergency service

AstraWatch provides automated, model-generated signals for educational and demonstration purposes. It does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of an alert.

  • Always verify detections with official agencies and local observations.
  • Do not use this prototype as the sole basis for evacuation or emergency decisions.
  • Users remain responsible for any action taken from displayed information.
DATA & PRIVACY

Built around open observation data

The prototype is designed to use publicly available Earth-observation data. This demo does not collect location information, personal data, account credentials or uploaded imagery.

  • Satellite-source terms and attribution remain applicable.
  • Operational deployments must use secure access controls and audit logs.
  • Any personal or sensitive response data must follow applicable law.
For official public warnings and emergency guidance, visit NDMA Sachet. In an emergency, follow instructions issued by local authorities.
