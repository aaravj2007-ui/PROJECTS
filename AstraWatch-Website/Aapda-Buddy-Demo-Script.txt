Aapda Buddy demo narration

1. “First, I enter my name and complete address so the Help & Safety tools know who needs assistance.”
2. “I select live location access and allow the browser permission.”
3. “Mission Control now combines official NDMA, National Centre for Seismology, INCOIS and USGS feeds.”
4. “The map and alert cards show current events, source details and severity.”
5. “I can ask Aapda AI a safety question by typing, pressing the search icon, or using Voice Command.”
6. “My spoken words are automatically written into the question box before the answer is requested.”
7. “Global History lets me search past earthquakes by location, year or magnitude.”
8. “In Help & Safety, my name and address are already filled into the SOS and safety check-in tools.”
9. “I can use GPS to update my location, send an SOS, or call 112 for immediate emergency help.”
10. “Finally, I verify every warning with the official agency before taking action.”
